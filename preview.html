What is ABA therapy?

ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis) is an evidence-based approach that helps children develop essential skills while reducing challenging behaviors through personalized, play-based learning.

Builds Communication

Finding their voice in ways that work for them

Develops Social Skills

Learning to connect and build friendships

Increases Independence

Building skills for everyday life activities

Emotional Regulation

Tools to manage big feelings in any situation
